## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **CSE student at Bangladesh University of Business & Technology (BUBT)**, Dhaka 🇧🇩. I build things at the intersection of **security, software and storytelling**, from IoT systems and Android apps to narrative-driven games.

- 🔐 **Web security:** hands-on PortSwigger labs (XSS, CSRF, CORS, CSP, SOP) with Burp Suite
- 📱 **Android:** Java, Firebase, SQLite and Gemini AI integrations
- 🔌 **IoT / hardware:** Arduino, ESP8266, LoRa, sensors, even PLC and relay control
- 🎮 **Games:** Python + Pygame, plus a custom engine in OpenGL
- 🌐 Portfolio source: [`utshokumarmondal`](https://github.com/UTSHOKUMARMONDAL/utshokumarmondal)
